RepFlow – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

RepFlow (“the app”) is a workout timer for exercises and routines. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: RepFlow does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only (in the app's private local storage): the exercises and routines you create, your favorites, your workout history and statistics, and your settings (language, theme, sound, vibration, countdown length, weekly goal). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, or earlier using the in-app “Delete all data” button on the Dashboard.

Backup files

The app can export your data to a file and import it back. The file is created and saved by you, where you choose. We never receive it.

Network access

The app works fully offline and does not send any data over the internet. The fonts and images it uses are included in the app.

Permissions

  • Vibration – to give haptic feedback during workouts (can be turned off in settings).
  • Keep screen awake – so the screen does not turn off while a workout is running.
  • Internet – declared by the app framework; the app does not use it to send or receive your data.

Children

RepFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health information

RepFlow is a timer and logbook. It does not measure health data, does not connect to health services, and does not give medical advice. Consult a professional before starting a new exercise program.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
